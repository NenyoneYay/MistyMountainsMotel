Our mountain

Rising above the valley, Misty Mountain earns its name from clouds that cling to its peaks year-round.

Rivertown

Nestled along winding waterways, Rivertown is a cozy fishing town turned cultural hotspot, where lively markets, local music, and dockside cafés invite tourists to relax, explore, and soak in its welcoming, small-town charm.

Skiing

Just beyond town, pristine slopes offer skiing and snowboarding for every skill level, from gentle beginner runs to challenging expert trails.

Hiking

Surrounded by scenic peaks, Rivertown offers abundant hiking trails for every skill level, enjoy the scenic views!
